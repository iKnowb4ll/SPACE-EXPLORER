My Dearest Momay,

To my Momay who means so much to me,

I don't think words will ever be enough to explain how much you mean to me, but I still want to try. There are so many things I want to tell you, so many feelings I want you to understand, and so many little things about you that I wish I could remind you of every single day.

First, I want you to know how thankful I am to have you in my life. Thank you for being someone who gives my days a little more meaning, someone who makes ordinary moments feel special, and someone who has become such an important part of my world. Even the simplest conversations with you can make my day better. Your presence means more to me than you probably realize.

I know I'm not perfect. There are times when I make mistakes, misunderstand things, overthink, or struggle to express what I truly feel. Sometimes I may not say the right words, and sometimes my actions might not reflect how much I care. But please know that I never want you to question whether you matter to me. You do, more than I can explain. I'm still learning how to become a better person, not only for myself but also for the people I genuinely care about.

I want to be someone you can feel comfortable with. Someone you can talk to when life gets overwhelming, someone who listens without judging, and someone who respects your feelings even when I don't completely understand them. You don't always have to pretend that everything is okay around me. You are allowed to have difficult days, to be quiet, to need time for yourself, and to express whatever is on your mind. I want you to know that your feelings matter, too.

I hope you never feel like you have to be perfect just to be appreciated. I appreciate you as you are, including the little things you might not even notice about yourself. You don't have to prove your worth to me. You deserve kindness, patience, respect, and genuine happiness.

I also want to see you achieve everything you've been dreaming about. I want to see you grow, discover new things, reach your goals, and become the person you've always wanted to be. Even when life gets difficult, I hope you never lose faith in yourself. I believe that you are capable of more than you sometimes think. Please don't forget to take care of yourself along the way. Your happiness, peace, and well-being matter.

If there's one thing I hope we can always remember, it's that love isn't about being perfect or having everything figured out. It's about understanding, patience, honesty, and choosing to treat each other with care. There will always be things we need to learn, and there will be moments when things aren't easy. What matters is how we handle them, how we communicate, and how willing we are to understand each other.

I don't want to make promises that are only beautiful to read. I want my actions to speak for me. I want to show you through the way I treat you, the way I listen, and the effort I make that you are someone worth appreciating. I want to celebrate your achievements, support you through difficult moments, and remind you that you are never less deserving of happiness just because you're having a bad day.

And lastly, I hope you never forget how special you are to me. No matter how busy life becomes, I hope you always find reasons to smile, moments to rest, and opportunities to become happier. Keep chasing your dreams, keep believing in yourself, and never be afraid to choose what brings you peace.

Thank you for being you. Thank you for the memories, the conversations, the laughter, and all the little moments that have become meaningful to me. If I could leave you with one message, it would be this: You are appreciated, you are valued, and you deserve a love that makes you feel safe, respected, and understood.

I care about you more than these paragraphs could ever express. And I hope that, through my words and especially through my actions, you never forget that.

Yours Truly,

Lotlot ♡
